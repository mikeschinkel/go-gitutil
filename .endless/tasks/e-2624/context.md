Decided by Mike on 2026-11-14 while planning go-goutil's extraction (E-2608, "Q1: C"). gomion's gommod/goutils/commit_signals.go classifies commit messages by the Conventional Commits spec: a `!` before the colon or a `BREAKING CHANGE:`/`BREAKING-CHANGE:` footer is breaking, `feat:` or `feat(scope):` is a feature, `fix:` likewise a fix, and the highest signal across the commits wins (breaking > feat > fix). That parsing knows nothing of Go, and go-gitutil's conventional_commit.go already holds the conventional-commit type table, so the classification belongs here, with the convention pluggable through a closure so a project using another prefix scheme can supply its own.
